# Marketing plan (owner request 2026-11-19: "read up on and execute the best practices")

Sources: 2026 local-ranking surveys and martial-arts marketing guides (see CHANGELOG 2026-11-19). The common findings,
in order of impact for a private-lesson school with no public schedule:

## 1. Google Business Profile (biggest lever for "tai chi san diego", "kung fu near me")
- ONE profile only: duplicates at the same business are a known ranking risk. Duplicate (13090909771712720109) to be
  removed via Google support, since "Remove business" leaves it on Maps.  OWNER
- Address shown (done, owner option 1), correct categories (Martial arts school + Tai chi school, done), accurate hours,
  description (done), website (done).
- Weekly Google post (offer: free first class; new blog post; a photo). AGENT drafts, owner approves the first few.
- Photos: 10+ real photos (training outdoors, forms, sword, teacher). OWNER supplies.
- A phone number on the profile raises calls/conversions. OWNER decides (none published yet).

## 2. Reviews (rating, steady volume, recency, detail; reply to every review; never gate or fake)
- Ask every current and past student for a Google review; mention the art and Santee/San Diego. OWNER sends the link.
- Reply to each review within a few days. AGENT drafts replies.
- Also collect on Superprof/lessons.com/Yelp.

## 3. Citations and links (consistent name/area everywhere; relevant industry links; "best of" lists)
- Lineage sites (littlenineheaven.org, littlenineheavenuk.com, .co.uk) to link to l9hkungfu.com. OWNER asks.
- Free listings: Bing Places (import from Google), Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Facebook page. OWNER creates accounts;
  AGENT fills in text.
- Pitch "best tai chi / kung fu in San Diego" list articles and local press. AGENT finds targets and drafts pitches.

## 4. Website (one page per service, local wording, fast, clear call to action)
- Done: per-system pages, San Diego titles, Q&A, structured data, 10 languages.
- Keep: weekly blog post; respond to inquiries fast (same day: speed of follow-up decides most enrollments). OWNER.

## 5. Content and video
- Short practice clips (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels) linked from the site and Google profile. OWNER films;
  AGENT writes titles/descriptions in English and Chinese.

## Measured every week (Semrush log + Search Console): impressions, clicks, map-pack views and calls on Google profile,
## reviews count, tracked keyword positions.
