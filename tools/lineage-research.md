## Grandmaster Chiao Chang-Hung = 喬長虹 (simplified 乔长虹)
Chinese name confirmed by owner 2026-11-05. Researched 2026-11-05 (owner asked to "analyze and remember").

Main source: Taiwanese academic profile, 張智瑋 (Taipei City Sports Dept.), 人物誌 第10輯
「武術界的蒲公英、武道推手：喬長虹」, bodyculture.org.tw (PDF):
https://bodyculture.org.tw/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/%E4%BA%BA%E7%89%A9%E8%AA%8C%E7%AC%AC10%E8%BC%AF-%E6%AD%A6%E8%A1%93%E7%95%8C%E7%9A%84%E8%92%B2%E5%85%AC%E8%8B%B1%E6%AD%A6%E9%81%93%E6%8E%A8%E6%89%8B-%E5%96%AC%E9%95%B7%E8%99%B9.pdf

- Born 2 Feb 1916 (民國5年) in 八面城, 昌圖縣, 遼寧省 (Liaoning). Died 2001, aged 85.
  (littlenineheaven.org gives 1914–2001; the birth year differs.)
- Only son; family had no martial background. Studied at a Japanese railway school from 1931 (age 15); later
  anti-Japanese underground work; 1945 head of the 中統局 office in Jinzhou; moved to Taiwan in 1949; later 調查局 督察長.
- Teacher: the abbot of 三清觀 (三清觀主) on 醫巫閭山 (Yiwulü Mountain, often shortened to 閭山 "Lüshan"),
  北鎮縣. Unnamed in the source; said to look about 50 but likely over 100. From him: fist, sword, internal
  training and 小九天武道. In 1948 (age 32) returned to him and began 洗髓神功.
  NOTE: the owner's ruling "Lushan Daoren" matches this: 閭山道人 = "the Taoist of Lü Mountain".
- Also studied 九宮劍法 in Japan.
- Arts: 洗髓神功 / 洗髓功 (signature), 小九天武道, external boxing, sword, 靜坐 meditation, 九宮劍法.
- Organizations: 中華民國國術會 (founding 1949; standing director and chief coach, terms 1–3); 臺灣省國術會 (1951);
  中國太極拳俱樂部 (1960, 總幹事); founded 中華全國九宮拳委員會 and 中華武道玄黃小九天社 (1969);
  菲華自強健身社, Philippines (1974, ~3 years). Chief judge at the 1st and 2nd 國際國術擂臺錦標賽 and the
  3rd 中華國術世界錦標賽 (Hawaii).
- 1971: honorary doctorate (source says Chinese University of Hong Kong). 1990: professor of Chinese at
  美國法界大學 (Dharma Realm Buddhist University), San Francisco.
- Books: 《中國洗髓功夫之真諦》; 《喬氏自家荷爾蒙健康長壽學別笈》 (四章堂, 2001); 《角觝》; 《武道史觀》;
  《小九天學真觧》; 《中華文化論衡》.
- Nickname: 「武術界的蒲公英」 ("dandelion of the martial arts world"): his students spread his arts worldwide.
- Students named: 談清雲 (chief disciple; 軒轅教 34th-generation head; 中華黃帝道丹功五術協會);
  麥克傑模 (American, head of the American branch of 小九天門, founded 中國小九天武道學校 in Los Angeles);
  史瓦滋 (French, 中國功夫館, Paris); 高三倫 (Malaysia, 洗髓功).
  CONFIRMED by owner 2026-11-05: 麥克傑模 (Màikè Jiémó; simplified 麦克杰模) IS Master James McNeil. So this
  Taiwanese source names McNeil as head of the American branch of 小九天門 and founder of a 小九天 school in Los Angeles.
- Biography: 《以身證道：中國洗髓功夫之真諦續篇 — 喬長虹傳》 by 談清雲.
- Generation numbering conflict: a Zhihu article titles him 「道家易筋洗髓經第三十三代掌門宗師」 (33rd).
  The owner ruled 2026-11-01 that Chiao is the 32nd generation and McNeil the 33rd. Keep the owner's ruling;
  told the owner about the difference.
