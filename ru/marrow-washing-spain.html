Sifu Ajay Kumra travels to Spain to teach Shih Shui (bone marrow washing, Xi Sui Gong 洗髓功) and Taoist lovemaking (fang chung shu) privately and in person. He comes to you: lessons take place where you are, one-on-one or in a small group.

The two practices belong together. Shih Shui is nei-kung: specific breathing methods, strengthening of the deep structures and directed energy circulation, with work on sexual energy at its centre. Taoist lovemaking is the classical art of conserving, transforming and circulating that same energy, the jing, instead of spending it carelessly.

Direct Transmission, Not a Book

Shih Shui has been widely and badly copied, and much of what circulates under its name is taught wrong by people who never received correction. Here it is passed on the way it always has been: in person, from teacher to student, stage by stage, with every detail corrected, as the masters before taught it. It is not learned from a book.

Линия преемственности

Sifu Ajay Kumra has practiced for 20 years and taught for 10, in the United States, Thailand and China. He trained under Master James McNeil (麥克傑模), Gate Keeper of the Little Nine Heaven Wu Tao system.

Master McNeil trained with Grandmaster Chiao Chang-Hung (喬長虹) in Taipei from 1984 until Chiao's death in 2001, and before that with Master Hsu Hong-Chi (許鴻基), "The Man With the Magic Hands". It was Grandmaster Chiao who chose to release Shih Shui beyond a handful of martial disciples.

Master McNeil learned the bedchamber arts from Master Hsu Hong-Chi, from Master Huang, and from four women in the mountains who were experts in them.

How It Works

  • Private and in person only: there are no video or online lessons.
  • One-on-one, as a couple, or in a small group.
  • Open to adult men and women. No martial arts experience is needed.
  • The length of the visit, the schedule and travel are arranged individually.

Write through the contact form or to info@l9hkungfu.com, say where you are and what you would like to learn, and the details are worked out with you personally.

Not in Spain?

Sifu Ajay travels to students anywhere in the world. He also teaches in Santee, California (San Diego County).

No. Shih Shui and Taoist lovemaking are taught in person only, so every detail can be corrected.
